# Positive Displacement Pump Types

The Hydraulic Institute standardizes rotodynamic and positive displacement [Link] pump types, definitions and nomenclature. The description for each rotodynamic pump type, and the related industry standard are included. 

Positive displacement pumps are divided into reciprocating types, which use pistons, plungers and diaphragms, and rotary types, which utilize meshing components, such as, screws, gears, lobes and vanes.
