# Positive Displacement Pump Types

Positive displacement (PD) pumps transfer liquid by repeatedly trapping and moving a defined volume from suction to discharge. Theoretical flow is determined by pump displacement and operating speed. Actual delivered flow can be lower because of internal leakage, called slip, and can also be affected by inlet conditions and fluid properties. The pump develops the differential pressure required by the system at the imposed flow, within its design limits. Because a PD pump continues to displace liquid when its discharge is restricted, the system requires suitable overpressure protection. PD pumps are broadly divided into reciprocating and rotary types.

## Reciprocating Pumps

Reciprocating PD pumps use linear motion of pistons, plungers, diaphragms, or bellows to draw liquid into and discharge liquid from pumping chambers. Their flow is inherently cyclic, so pulsation, inlet piping, acceleration pressure, valve behavior, and pressure protection are important application considerations. The four categories introduced here are air-operated, steam, power, and controlled-volume pumps.

## Rotary Pumps

A rotary PD pump has a pumping chamber containing rotating elements, such as gears, screws, vanes, pistons, or lobes. The rotating elements act with close running clearances and do not use separate inlet and discharge valves as part of the pumping principle. The main rotary types introduced here are sliding vane, axial piston, flexible member, lobe, gear, circumferential piston, flexible member, and screw pumps.

Refer to <a href="https://www.pumps.org/what-we-do/standards/?pumps-search-product=ANSI%2FHI+3.1-3.5&hi-order=asc&hi-order-by=name" target="_blank">ANSI/HI 3.1-3.5</a>, <a href="https://www.pumps.org/product/ansi-hi-3-6-rotary-pump-tests/" target="_blank">ANSI/HI 3.6</a>, <a href="https://www.pumps.org/what-we-do/standards/?pumps-search-product=ANSI%2FHI+4.1-4.6&hi-order=asc&hi-order-by=name" target="_blank">ANSI/HI 4.1-4.6</a>, and <a href="https://www.pumps.org/what-we-do/standards/?pumps-search-product=9.6.9&hi-order=asc&hi-order-by=name" target="_blank">ANSI/HI 9.6.9</a> for additional information on rotary PD pumps.
